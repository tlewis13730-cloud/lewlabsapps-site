Support

Questions, a problem to report, or an idea for Lew Labs Apps? We can help.

Simple Checklist

For list, widget, sharing, or Pro AI Create help, write to support@lewlabsapps.com. Include your Android device, app version, what you tried, and what happened. Do not send private list content unless it is needed to explain the issue and you choose to share it.

Where are my lists?

Core checklist data is stored on your device. No Lew Labs account is needed.

Can I work offline?

Core list editing and widgets work offline. AI Create, ads, and some device speech services need a connection.

How do I add or change a widget?

Use the widget control in Simple Checklist or Android's home-screen Widgets menu. Open the widget's settings to assign it to another list.

Where are the lessons?

Open the in-app Quick Tour, Learn Simple Checklist, Full App Tutorial, or individual feature lessons.

How does AI Create save a list?

Scan or describe a list, review and edit the result, then save to a new list or add selected items to an existing list.

How do I remove my lists?

Delete lists in the app, or clear its Android app data. Android backup settings may affect restored copies.

Simple Family Games

The Simple Family Games support page has game-specific help, including Save & Continue, Family Game Night, and bug-report details. For direct help, email hello@lewlabsapps.com.

Privacy or general contact

Read the Simple Checklist privacy policy or Simple Family Games privacy policy. Privacy questions go to privacy@lewlabsapps.com; general questions and feedback go to hello@lewlabsapps.com.
