Optional Pro

AI Create, with you in control

Pro removes banner ads and adds Scan a List and Describe a List. AI suggestions are reviewed before they become checklist items.

Scan a List

Take or choose a photo of a handwritten or printed list. AI reads it, then you edit and review the items before saving them to a new or existing list.

Describe a List

Hold to record, release, and review an editable transcript. Choose Make My List to generate checklist items, review them, then save to a new or existing list. You can also type instead of recording.

Pro subscriptions are planned and are not currently sold. Optional AI tools require a connection. Core checklist use does not require one. Availability and usage limits are shown in the app.

Plugin in development · Private testing

A Simple Checklist plugin for ChatGPT is on the way.

The plugin is being tested privately and is not yet publicly listed in ChatGPT. When connected, it can prepare a link from checklist items in your conversation. The Android app already supports reviewing and importing this Free handoff. The plugin cannot see lists already on your phone or change them remotely.

1. Prepare a list

Ask ChatGPT for checklist items. The connected tool validates the suggestions and returns a link; it does not create a list in the app.

2. Open the link

Open the link on an Android device with a supported Simple Checklist version to reach an editable review. If it opens in a browser, see the handoff help below.

3. Choose and approve

Edit or leave out items, choose a new or existing list, then approve. Canceling leaves your lists unchanged.

There is no public plugin availability date yet. This handoff is Free and separate from Pro Scan a List and Describe a List. Checklist text in a handoff link is encoded, not encrypted, and links do not expire. Reopening asks for fresh approval and can add duplicate items; share links carefully.
