Simple Checklist

Make a list once. Use it whenever you need it. Core checklists stay on your device; optional Pro tools help turn a photo or description into a list.

Explore featuresRead the privacy policy

Everyday list making

The core app is built for repeatable tasks without an account.

Reusable lists

Create multiple lists, check and uncheck items, add several items at once, and reorder lists or items.

Fast ways to add

Use voice add, import shared text or lists, and share a list when someone else needs it.

Widgets and views

Keep lists on your Android home screen, reassign a widget to another list, and use View / Sort to find the layout you need.

Learn as you go

Start with the in-app Quick Tour, then open Learn Simple Checklist, the Full App Tutorial, or focused feature lessons.

Optional Pro

AI Create, with you in control

Pro removes banner ads and adds Scan a List and Describe a List. AI suggestions are reviewed before they become checklist items.

Scan a List

Take or choose a photo of a handwritten or printed list. AI reads it, then you edit and review the items before saving them to a new or existing list.

Describe a List

Hold to record, release, and review an editable transcript. Choose Make My List to generate checklist items, review them, then save to a new or existing list. You can also type instead of recording.

Optional AI tools require a connection. Core checklist use does not require one. Availability and usage limits are shown in the app.
